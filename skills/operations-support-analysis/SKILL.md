# Operations and Support Analysis

Terminology: name every component, service, dependency and interface with `australian-it-ot-terminology` (`.agents/skills/australian-it-ot-terminology/SKILL.md`), classify it as OT, supporting IT, shared, platform or external, and write in Australian English.

Establish who performs each operational responsibility and what process or tool supports it:

- business, service, application, infrastructure, network, security, and vendor ownership;
- support tiers, hours, locations, escalation, and on-call arrangements;
- monitoring, alerting, logging, health checks, and event response;
- incident, problem, change, release, maintenance, and outage processes;
- patching, vulnerability management, antivirus or Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR), and exception handling;
- certificate, account, licence, capacity, backup, and lifecycle maintenance;
- runbooks, architecture records, configuration records, and support contracts.

Separate formal process, documented process, reported practice, and observed tool configuration. Do not state that a control is effective merely because a product or policy exists.

Return a responsibility matrix, operating-process summary, tooling/evidence references, constraints, and unresolved ownership gaps.
