## Analysis frame (F1)

If the card has an "Analysis frame", end the sheet with a `## F1 Analysis frame` block: one line per slot (`- S3: B2, B3`) naming the blocks whose text answers it, or `- S9: QUESTION: <what is not known>`. Cite the frame's evidence IDs in those blocks. A "Likely" note is never a fact: turn what it asks you to confirm into a `QUESTION:` line.
