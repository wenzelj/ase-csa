## Findings blocks

- Findings blocks: `Bullets:` with one finding per bullet.
- DUPLICATE_BULLET: say each finding once. Two bullets that name the same hosts and cite the same evidence are one finding.
- REPEATS_TABLE: the framework places a table above the bullets (a host-by-host list of what the frame covers). Do not restate it; write only findings the table cannot show.
- SHARE_ACCESS_MISMATCH: give a share's access as its "Who may open the shares" row records it, share by share: Read and Full are different findings.
- NAME_NOT_RECORDED, UNNAMED_PEER: name a peer known only by address by that address alone; never say its name, zone or role is unrecorded or unknown.
- OUT_OF_SCOPE: every bullet must concern the frame's topic; leave out findings that belong to another subsection.
- ACRONYM_EXPANSION: expand a project acronym only as the card's "Project acronyms" list gives it, or leave it unexpanded; never invent a meaning.
- REGISTER_ROW_MISSING: a register built from a list on the card has a row for every name on that list.
- REGISTER_HOST_WRONG, REGISTER_HOST_MISSING: a register row that names hosts names exactly the hosts the card's lines give for that name, no more and no fewer.
- TEXT_TOO_LONG: keep a subsection's text within the sentence limit its frame states; the table and its notes carry the detail.
