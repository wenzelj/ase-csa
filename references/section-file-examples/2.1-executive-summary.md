## Summary

The assessed application runs on a small estate of servers and workstations that were captured through the discovery scripts described in Appendix B. Most operational functions depend on enterprise services shared with the rest of the organisation.

The main risks found in this assessment concern shared administrative access, limited time synchronisation outside the enterprise, and a small number of hosts that have not been captured yet.
